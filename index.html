Smart School is a school-management platform: attendance, fees, timetables, exams, report cards, ID cards, a parent app and more, built with Rails, a Vue single-page app and a thin Android shell.

These notes are written as the app evolves. Each one covers a real problem we hit, how we measured it, what we changed and what we decided not to do, with the numbers before and after.
